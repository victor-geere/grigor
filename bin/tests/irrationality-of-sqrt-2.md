**Theorem**: √2 is irrational.

**Proof** (by contradiction):

Assume √2 = p/q, where p and q are coprime positive integers (q ≠ 0).

Then p² = 2q²

⇒ p² is even ⇒ p is even (let p = 2m)

Substitute: (2m)² = 2q
           4m² = 2q²
           2m² = q²

⇒ q² is even ⇒ q is even.

But if both p and q are even, they share a common factor of 2, contradicting that they are coprime.

Therefore, √2 is irrational.
